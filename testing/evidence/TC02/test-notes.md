# TC02 – Incomplete Referral / Clarification Pathway

## Scenario
An incomplete referral enters the Riverside operational process and is directed towards referral clarification and review.

## Purpose
To evaluate the referral completeness and clarification pathway, including the identification of incomplete referral information and the associated clarification task.

## Expected Result
Where required referral information is incomplete, the process should direct the case towards referral clarification before the referral proceeds through the normal operational pathway.

## Actual Result
The available execution evidence shows the referral review and clarification stages within the process, including:
- ENTER / REVIEW REFERRAL DETAILS
- REQUEST REFERRAL CLARIFICATION

## Status
PARTIAL EVIDENCE

## Evidence

## Defects

## Corrective Action
Not applicable to the available evidence.

## Retest
A complete end-to-end clarification-loop retest may be required to demonstrate the full gateway transition and return to the normal referral pathway.

## Limitations
